# Product Boundaries

## Buyer, promise, and outputs

The buyers are local SEO agencies and freelancers, multi-location operators, and in-house storefront, hybrid, and service-area operators. The promise is source-cited memory and synthesis that turns approved exports and current doctrine into health evidence, a risk register, an action roadmap, a weekly report, and an approval queue.

## Read-only boundary

The product is read-only for external systems. It does not edit Google Business Profile, submit posts or Q&A, reply to reviews, change Search Console or GA4, submit citations, send messages, publish pages, or call paid enrichment APIs. Repository-local generation is allowed only with explicit targets, deterministic inputs, and verification.

## Refusals

- No credentials, tokens, cookies, or private client data in repository artifacts. Client vaults may contain operator-authorized raw exports and must remain access-controlled.
- No fabricated score, metric, ranking, weight, traffic share, cadence, source, quote, test, or date.
- No address gaming, virtual-office abuse, fake or review-gated reviews, PBN tactics, doorway pages, hidden text, or guaranteed ranking language.
- No prohibited permanent storage of Google Places content.
- No recommendation without a source, confidence, owner, and rollback when action is reversible.
- No unsupported model, API, UI, or feature status presented as universal.

## Measurement boundary

The product may calculate transparent values directly from supplied fixtures or exports, such as row counts, rating distributions, review dates, geo-grid rank summaries, and source coverage. The citation adapter does not currently calculate a NAP difference. It does not infer causal ranking impact. A score or grade is allowed only when its formula, inputs, no-data behavior, and tests are documented. Until then, the Health Scorecard reports evidence state rather than a numeric or letter grade.

## Evidence boundary

The historical course export is retained as provenance, not current authority. Current advice is controlled by the canonical source and claim ledgers plus deterministic overlays. `EVIDENCE-BASED`, `PRACTITIONER`, `CONTESTED`, and `FOLKLORE` describe evidence type; they do not substitute for an assessment or citation.

## Privacy and licensing

Raw client exports remain local and are excluded from public packages. Derived review notes omit reviewer names and per-review identity; raw exports can still contain personal data. The proprietary license and third-party notices control redistribution. Public artifacts contain only reviewed summaries, source links, compliant brief excerpts, and generated sample data.
