## Working guidance

Evaluate AI Search visibility only within a recorded platform, locale, query set, and collection window. Citation breadth and answer influence are separate observations.

## Use this note

Inputs: The platform/model, reproducible prompt or query, locale, date, responses, and collection method.

Result: Separate citation-selection and answer-influence observations, with experimental limitations and no universal factor weights.

## Evidence and limits

Generative-engine optimization experiments show bounded, domain- and platform-dependent effects; citation breadth and answer influence are distinct, so universal GEO recipes are not established. (Claim: geo-tactics-provisional; sources: geo-kdd-2024, geo-citation-absorption-2026.)

Fixed AI citation counts, Reddit citation-share percentages, and guaranteed local AIO tactics are unsupported across platforms, query classes, locales, and dates. (Claim: ai-citation-formulas-unsupported; contrary evidence: google-ai-features, geo-citation-absorption-2026.)
