## Task

Separate observed listing facts from gaps that require further collection. Propose primary-source checks and real directories relevant to the business; do not fabricate listings or perform submissions.

## Required inputs

Confirmed business information, authorized listing sample, locale, and research question.

## Output and acceptance

A research brief with observed evidence, open questions, exact proposed sources, and collection boundaries.

Tie factual assertions to supplied source IDs and locators. Retain needs_input for missing facts. The operator reviews scope, factual support, and privacy; any proposed external change needs owner approval and rollback.
