> Draft prompt for [[creating-an-audience-avatar|Creating an Audience Avatar]]. Use only authorized evidence and review the result before external use.

## Task

Summarize observed needs, barriers, and decision questions. Distinguish recurring evidence from a single observation and from a proposed segment; avoid invented demographics or quotes.

## Required inputs

Authorized interviews, customer questions, and analytics with dates and collection scope.

## Output and acceptance

Audience needs summary, evidence table, hypotheses, and unanswered research questions.

Tie factual assertions to supplied source IDs and locators. Retain needs_input for missing facts. The operator reviews scope, factual support, and privacy; any proposed external change needs owner approval and rollback.
