## Task

Combine the approved sections in outline order. Remove repetition, resolve transitions, and preserve citations. Do not add new claims while assembling.

## Required inputs

Approved outline, reviewed section drafts, source table, and editorial decisions.

## Output and acceptance

One coherent Draft plus an assembly change log and unresolved inconsistencies.
