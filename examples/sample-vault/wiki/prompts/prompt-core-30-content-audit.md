## Task

Map each page to a useful customer task. Identify duplicate coverage and unsupported location pages; treat Core 30 as an optional historical planning label, not a required count or title formula.

## Required inputs

Current URL inventory, confirmed services/locations, observed customer questions, and content evidence.

## Output and acceptance
