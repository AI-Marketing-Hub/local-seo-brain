## Task

Identify the visitor’s main task and factual gaps. Rewrite the explanation of the business and next action; retain useful evidence and remove invented credentials, testimonials, or benefits.

## Required inputs

Current homepage, owner-confirmed services/service area, contact facts, and supporting evidence.

## Output and acceptance

Homepage Draft, section-purpose map, assertion/source table, and owner questions.

Tie factual assertions to supplied source IDs and locators. Retain needs_input for missing facts. The operator reviews scope, factual support, and privacy; any proposed external change needs owner approval and rollback.
