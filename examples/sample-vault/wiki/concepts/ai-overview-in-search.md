# AI Overview in Search

## Evidence status

- Assessment: **contested**
- Confidence: **CONTESTED**
- Verified: **2026-10-15**
- Refresh due: **2026-11-14**
- Claim IDs: ai-features-no-special-requirements, ai-summary-click-direction, ai-citation-formulas-unsupported, search-console-genai-reporting, operator-evidence-review

## Working guidance

Check ordinary Search eligibility and supplied AI-feature impression reports. Report the measured scope, missing data, and observational limits.

## Use this note

Inputs: The named AI Search surface, query or report, locale, device, collection date, and website eligibility evidence.

Result: A scoped eligibility or observation record, report limitations, and missing data without a universal click-loss estimate.

## Evidence and limits

Google documents no additional technical requirements, mandatory special schema, or AI text file for eligibility in AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond ordinary Search requirements and policies. (Claim: ai-features-no-special-requirements; sources: google-ai-features, google-succeeding-ai-search.)

Two independent observational datasets associate AI-summary result pages with fewer clicks to external/traditional results; no single percentage should be generalized across query sets. (Claim: ai-summary-click-direction; sources: pew-ai-summary-click-study, ahrefs-ai-overview-ctr-study.)

Fixed AI citation counts, Reddit citation-share percentages, and guaranteed local AIO tactics are unsupported across platforms, query classes, locales, and dates. (Claim: ai-citation-formulas-unsupported; contrary evidence: google-ai-features, geo-citation-absorption-2026.)

Google documents worldwide availability of Search Console Generative AI performance reporting as of 2026-08-31. It exposes impressions for AI Overviews and AI Mode by page, country, date, and device; dedicated AI-feature click or query reporting is not established by the reviewed documentation. (Claim: search-console-genai-reporting; sources: google-gen-ai-report-launch, google-gen-ai-performance-help.)
