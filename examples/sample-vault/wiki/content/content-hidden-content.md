# Content: Hidden Content

## Evidence status

- Assessment: **accepted**
- Confidence: **EVIDENCE-BASED**
- Verified: **2026-10-15**
- Refresh due: **2026-11-14**
- Claim IDs: hidden-and-doorway-abuse, accordions-allowed, operator-evidence-review

## Working guidance

Use user-facing tabs and accordions for navigation when helpful. Do not hide text or create doorway pages to manipulate ranking.

## Use this note

Inputs: Rendered page states, relevant HTML/CSS, and the user task served by any hidden or expandable content.

Result: An observed visibility/accessibility record distinguishing useful controls from suspected manipulation and identifying the next check.

## Evidence and limits

Hidden text used to manipulate ranking, doorway pages, and scaled low-value location/content pages violate Google's spam policies. (Claim: hidden-and-doorway-abuse; sources: google-search-spam-policies.)

User-facing accordions and tabs are not automatically hidden-content abuse when they serve ordinary design and user-experience purposes. (Claim: accordions-allowed; sources: google-search-spam-policies.)
