## Working guidance

Draft from the approved outline and supplied sources. Fact-check claims, metadata, and image descriptions; remove invented experience or filler.

## Use this note

Inputs: The approved outline, source passages, owner-confirmed business facts, and authorized media.

Result: A Draft article with attributable claims, checked metadata and image descriptions, and a fact-review list.

## Evidence and limits

Google documents no preferred universal word count, keyword density, publication cadence, or fixed page-count framework; content should be people-first and useful for its purpose. (Claim: no-universal-content-formula; sources: google-helpful-content, google-seo-starter-guide.)

Prompts and workflows must not fabricate firsthand experiences, identities, costs, awards, guarantees, reviews, or community posts. (Claim: truthful-firsthand-content; sources: google-maps-prohibited-content, local-seo-brain-product-contract.)

Google permits generative AI as a content-production aid. Generating many pages without user value may violate scaled-content-abuse policy; content and metadata should be manually checked for accuracy and trustworthiness, with creation context when useful to readers. (Claim: generative-ai-content-quality; sources: google-generative-ai-content-guidance, google-helpful-content.)
