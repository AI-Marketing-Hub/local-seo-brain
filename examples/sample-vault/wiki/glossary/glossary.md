## Working guidance

Use service-area business for a business that visits or delivers to customers; distinguish it from a customer-facing storefront or hybrid. Keep unresolved account details marked needs_input.

## Evidence and limits

A service-area business that does not serve customers at its address should remove or hide that address; storefront eligibility requires a real customer-facing location. (Claim: gbp-address-service-area; sources: google-gbp-representation-guidelines, google-gbp-service-areas.)
