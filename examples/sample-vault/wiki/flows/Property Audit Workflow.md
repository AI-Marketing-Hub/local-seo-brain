# Property Review Workflow

## Preconditions

- Obtain an authorized crawl, rendered page, or manual evidence file.
- Confirm the business facts and target locale.
- Read the relevant note's Evidence status; historical audit formulas are not current authority.

## Review surfaces

1. Crawlability, indexability, canonicalization, rendering, and structured-data validity.
2. People-first usefulness, factual accuracy, service/location fit, and first-hand evidence.
3. Titles, descriptions, headings, and links without fixed length, density, or link-count formulas.
4. Page experience as one scoped system, never a ranking guarantee.
5. Structured data as a representation format, not a guaranteed result or AI inclusion mechanism.
6. Existing URLs and earned links that require preservation before a proposed change.

## Output boundary

Record observed evidence and unknowns. Do not invent numeric health scores, impact-by-effort ranks, traffic forecasts, or causal lifts. The bundled [[Health Scorecard]] remains Draft. Proposed website changes require an [[Approval Queue]] record and rollback.

## Refuse

No hidden text for ranking, doorway or scaled low-value location pages, fabricated experience, link schemes, or guaranteed outcomes.
