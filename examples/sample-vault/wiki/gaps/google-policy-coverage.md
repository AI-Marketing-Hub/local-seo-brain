# Google Policy Coverage

## Covered in the active ledgers

- Business Profile eligibility, representation, name, categories, storefront, service-area, virtual-office, coworking, verification, and review-manipulation rules
- local ranking guidance and the absence of public universal weights
- Search spam, helpful-content, link, structured-data, AI-feature, page-experience, and Search Console reporting doctrine
- Business Profile Q&A API discontinuation versus contested manual UI status
- Maps/Places content, attribution, storage, caching, and privacy boundaries at the product-refusal level

## Deferred or locale-dependent

- category and feature availability by country, industry, account, device, and rollout cohort
- suspension, restriction, appeal, and reinstatement outcomes for a specific account
- UI-only changes without stable official documentation
- paid product integrations or live API capabilities not shipped by this repository

Deferred items remain `needs_input`, provisional, or contested. They must not be generalized from one account or screenshot.
