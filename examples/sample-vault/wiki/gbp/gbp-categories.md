## Working guidance

Choose the most specific category describing the confirmed core business. Add categories only when accurate; record potential reverification and owner approval.

## Use this note

Inputs: The confirmed core business, current categories, and current category/representation guidance.

Result: Accurate category candidates with their business evidence, change risks, owner decision, and rollback.

## Evidence and limits

A Business Profile should use the real-world name and the fewest accurate categories needed to describe its core business; fixed category quotas are not official doctrine. (Claim: gbp-name-and-categories; sources: google-gbp-representation-guidelines, google-gbp-categories.)
