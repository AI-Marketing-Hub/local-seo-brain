## Working guidance

Record the exact proposed diff, source, owner decision, and rollback before an external change. Check current verification guidance for the actual profile; do not invent suspension rates or assume a method is available.

## Use this note

Inputs: The exact current/proposed fields, controlling policy, owner decision, and available rollback records.

Result: A reviewable change request identifying scope, risk, verification uncertainty, approval, and rollback.

## Evidence and limits

Local SEO Brain is advisory: any account control or third-party mutation requires explicit owner approval, scoped execution, and a rollback path. (Claim: product-account-mutations-require-approval; sources: local-seo-brain-product-contract.)

Verification evidence must truthfully demonstrate business location, existence, and management. Video verification must be captured live, unique and unedited, and exclude sensitive information and other people’s faces. (Claim: gbp-verification-authenticity; sources: google-gbp-verification, google-gbp-video-verification.)
