# 2026 Google Business Profile State

The API facts below document platform fields only. Local SEO Brain imports exports and has no live account integration. Use [[gbp-overview]] and [[GBP Optimization Workflow]] for the operator review.

## Current evidence

A service-area business that does not serve customers at its address should remove or hide that address; storefront eligibility requires a real customer-facing location. (Claim: gbp-address-service-area; sources: google-gbp-representation-guidelines, google-gbp-service-areas.)

A rented mailing or virtual-office address where the business does not operate is ineligible; a coworking storefront requires permanent signage, customer reception, and the business's own staff during stated hours. (Claim: gbp-virtual-office-coworking; sources: google-gbp-representation-guidelines.)

A Business Profile should use the real-world name and the fewest accurate categories needed to describe its core business; fixed category quotas are not official doctrine. (Claim: gbp-name-and-categories; sources: google-gbp-representation-guidelines, google-gbp-categories.)

Verification evidence must truthfully demonstrate business location, existence, and management. Video verification must be captured live, unique and unedited, and exclude sensitive information and other people’s faces. (Claim: gbp-verification-authenticity; sources: google-gbp-verification, google-gbp-video-verification.)

Google Maps and Business Profile reviews prohibit incentives for posting, revising, or removing reviews, even when disclosed. Fake, gated, quota-driven, conflict-of-interest, selectively positive, or wording-manipulated reviews are prohibited. (Claim: review-manipulation-prohibited; sources: google-maps-prohibited-content, google-maps-ugc-policy, google-gbp-get-reviews.)

The My Business Q&A API was discontinued on 2025-11-03, but that does not establish a universal sunset of consumer place Q&A or prove Ask Maps replaced it everywhere. (Claim: qanda-api-sunset-manual-contested; sources: google-gbp-qanda-api-changelog.)

Ask Maps launched as a conversational place-discovery experience with an initial U.S. and India mobile rollout; Google did not document it as a universal replacement for Business Profile Q&A. (Claim: ask-maps-scope; sources: google-maps-ask-maps-launch.)

From 2026-07-24, My Business API v4.9 documents reviewReplyUrl retrieval through reviews get, list, and batchGetReviews. (Claim: gbp-api-review-reply-url; sources: google-gbp-api-changelog.)

From 2026-10-09, My Business API v4.9 documents GLUTEN_FREE and DAIRY_FREE DietaryRestriction values for food-menu items through getFoodMenus and updateFoodMenus. (Claim: gbp-api-food-menu-dietary-restrictions; sources: google-gbp-api-changelog.)
