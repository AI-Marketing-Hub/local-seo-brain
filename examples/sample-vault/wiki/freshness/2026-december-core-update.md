# Search Update Timeline Through October 2026

Use the official status history for event dates. Compare actual page and query evidence before diagnosing change; an update label does not explain a client outcome. See [[key-google-rank-factors]].

## Current evidence

The Search Status Dashboard records August and September 2026 spam updates in addition to earlier named events. Rollout dates alone do not establish why an individual site moved. (Claim: search-update-events; sources: google-search-status-summary, google-core-updates.)

Aggregated interaction data has been described in primary trial evidence, but this does not validate GA bounce-rate formulas, dwell-time thresholds, CTR manipulation, or local-ranking guarantees. (Claim: behavior-formulas-unsupported; contrary evidence: doj-google-interaction-testimony.)

## Regional Search boundaries

Google aggregator and supplier units support EEA local-business queries. Aggregators require approved vertical-search-service status and data integration. Suppliers are direct providers serving EEA users and appear only alongside an aggregator unit. (Claim: eea-local-business-search-units; sources: google-search-aggregator-unit, google-search-supplier-unit.)

From 2026-08-30, site-reputation-policy manual-action effects differ by searcher location. Outside the EEA the affected portion can be directly impacted; inside the EEA that manual-action impact does not apply, though the portion may be separated and ranked independently. (Claim: eea-site-reputation-enforcement; sources: google-site-reputation-eea-update-2026, google-search-spam-policies.)
