# Provider-Neutral AI Evaluation

Before using a provider, verify its current capabilities, model identifier, privacy terms, and authorized input class. Record task, date, prompt version, observed limitations, and human reviewer. See [[which-ai-tools-to-use]].

## Current evidence

The operator workflow is to inventory authorized evidence, separate observed facts from hypotheses, draft a scoped finding with sources and missing inputs, and obtain owner approval and a rollback plan for any proposed external change. (Claim: operator-evidence-review; sources: local-seo-brain-product-contract.)

Derived review notes omit review-author identity; authorized raw exports remain immutable and access-controlled rather than being copied into public deliverables. (Claim: product-review-privacy; sources: local-seo-brain-product-contract.)
