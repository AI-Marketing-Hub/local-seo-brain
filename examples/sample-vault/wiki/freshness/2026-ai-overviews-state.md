# 2026 AI Search State

Review the region, surface, reporting dimensions, collection window, and missing-data limits before using an AI Search observation. Use [[ai-overview-in-search]] for the working checklist.

## Current evidence

Google documents no additional technical requirements, mandatory special schema, or AI text file for eligibility in AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond ordinary Search requirements and policies. (Claim: ai-features-no-special-requirements; sources: google-ai-features, google-succeeding-ai-search.)

Two independent observational datasets associate AI-summary result pages with fewer clicks to external/traditional results; no single percentage should be generalized across query sets. (Claim: ai-summary-click-direction; sources: pew-ai-summary-click-study, ahrefs-ai-overview-ctr-study.)

Google documents worldwide availability of Search Console Generative AI performance reporting as of 2026-08-31. It exposes impressions for AI Overviews and AI Mode by page, country, date, and device; dedicated AI-feature click or query reporting is not established by the reviewed documentation. (Claim: search-console-genai-reporting; sources: google-gen-ai-report-launch, google-gen-ai-performance-help.)

Google permits generative AI as a content-production aid. Generating many pages without user value may violate scaled-content-abuse policy; content and metadata should be manually checked for accuracy and trustworthiness, with creation context when useful to readers. (Claim: generative-ai-content-quality; sources: google-generative-ai-content-guidance, google-helpful-content.)
