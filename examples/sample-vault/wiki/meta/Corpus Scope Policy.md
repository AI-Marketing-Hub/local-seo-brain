# Corpus Scope Policy

## Operating contract

The corpus contains licensed historical seed material, current official doctrine, scoped independent research, operator workflows, fixtures, and generated projections. Inclusion does not make a source current authority. Every material statement needs a claim disposition and confidence tag.

Client exports belong only in access-controlled client vaults. Credentials, reviewer identity, private transaction records, and restricted Places payloads are outside the repository corpus.
