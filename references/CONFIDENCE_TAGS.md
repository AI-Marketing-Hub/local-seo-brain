# Confidence Tags

Every material domain claim carries exactly one evidence-type tag in addition to its assessment.

- `EVIDENCE-BASED`: supported by authoritative primary data, controlling official documentation, or reproducible research appropriate to the statement.
- `PRACTITIONER`: useful operational judgment whose causal effect is not established.
- `CONTESTED`: credible evidence conflicts or the claim varies materially by scope.
- `FOLKLORE`: popular advice without adequate support; retained only to explain or deprecate it.

Assessments are separate: `accepted`, `provisional`, `contested`, `unsupported`, or `deprecated`. A high-risk accepted empirical claim requires two active sources with different `independence_key` values. Direct official policy may use the controlling policy page alone unless an enforcement or outcome inference is added.

Quotes must be brief, verified, and attributed. Otherwise write a labeled paraphrase. Unknown values remain `null`, `unsupported`, `no_data`, or `needs_input`.
