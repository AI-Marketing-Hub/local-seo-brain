## Independence rules

Separate Google pages do not become independent corroboration of Google’s own product claims. The Search Central, Search Console, Business Profile, Maps content-policy, Maps Platform, and status surfaces retain distinct keys for change monitoring, but a high-risk empirical conclusion still needs independent origins. Pew and Ahrefs satisfy that rule only for the directional AI-summary click claim. Google announcements and help pages are not counted as independent of each other.

## Exclusions

No full third-party pages are committed. Snippets, search-result text, worker drafts, social posts, affiliate pages, opaque vendor surveys, and unverifiable screenshots are not canonical evidence. Provider model lineups are excluded from core doctrine because their maintenance cost exceeds their local-SEO decision value.
