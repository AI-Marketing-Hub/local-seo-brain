# Business Profile Representation and Eligibility

## Distilled doctrine

Business Profile eligibility depends on a real business that makes in-person contact with customers during stated hours. A service-area business that does not serve customers at its address should hide it. A virtual office is ineligible; a coworking storefront needs permanent signage, customer reception, and the business's own staff during stated hours.

Names and categories should represent the real-world business. Verification material must be authentic. Video verification should not expose sensitive identity documents or stage a false operating presence.
