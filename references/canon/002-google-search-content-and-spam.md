# Search Content and Spam Policy

## Distilled doctrine

Google documents people-first usefulness rather than a universal word count, keyword density, page count, publication cadence, or fixed link count. Links should be crawlable, contextual, useful, and appropriately qualified.

Hidden text used to manipulate ranking, doorway or scaled low-value pages, PBNs, paid ranking links without qualification, and optimized forum/comment links violate Search spam policy. Ordinary user-facing accordions are not automatically hidden-text abuse.
