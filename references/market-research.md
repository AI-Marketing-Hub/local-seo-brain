# Independent Market and Research Evidence

Primary research re-reviewed 2026-10-15. This is a literature review, not a new measurement or experiment. Official doctrine answers platform requirements; independent sources are used only for empirical questions that policy cannot answer.

## Supported, narrowly

### AI summaries and external clicks

Pew Research Center analyzed 68,879 Google searches from 900 U.S. adults in March 2025 and found fewer traditional-result clicks when an AI summary appeared. The reviewed Ahrefs 2026 rerun compared desktop Search Console CTR for informational queries and also reported a decline. Its previous estimate is superseded in part; neither estimate becomes a universal loss rate. The methods, populations, and counterfactuals differ, so the accepted result is directional only. A universal loss percentage is not supported.

### Generative-engine optimization

The KDD 2024 GEO work demonstrated bounded benchmark gains on synthetic and post-retrieval setups, varying by domain and then-current engine. A 2026 preprint separately distinguished citation selection from answer-level citation absorption across 602 prompts. These studies justify controlled experiments, not a universal local-SEO recipe, fixed citation count, or mandatory Reddit program.

### Reviews and consumer demand

Michael Luca’s Yelp study estimated a causal revenue effect for independent restaurants in a historical 2003-2009 Seattle/Washington State sample. It does not establish Google local-ranking causality, apply to chains or every industry, or justify a universal review target. Google’s own local-ranking help says review count and positive ratings can help, but publishes no weight.

### Interaction data

DOJ trial material describes aggregated interaction data and NavBoost. It does not validate a site owner’s Analytics bounce rate, a dwell-time threshold, CTR manipulation, or a mechanical local-ranking formula. Those operational claims remain unsupported.

## Unsupported or deprecated

- exact zero-click share across all searches;
- fixed local ranking weights, review thresholds, decay curves, or category weights;
- universal GBP post/photo/reply cadence;
- EXIF GPS or image geotagging as a local-ranking lever;
- structured data as direct ranking or AI-citation causality;
- exact keyword density, word count, internal-link count, or anchor ratio;
- PBNs, paid insertions, or comment/forum links as safe ranking tactics;
- bounce-rate or CTR manipulation;
- fixed Reddit or LLM citation share;
- a universal number of sources in an AI Overview.

## Method limitations

SERP and model behavior is volatile. Observational datasets are vulnerable to query-selection, device, locale, time-window, and counterfactual choices. Vendor studies may carry product incentives. Preprints may not be peer reviewed. Platform policy is authoritative for eligibility and prohibited behavior, not independent evidence of effectiveness. Every empirical use must retain the source’s sample, window, method, sponsor, and limitations.

## Stop decision

The maintenance pass re-inspected the ledger inventory. Remaining high-risk formula claims lack transparent independent evidence; repeated dependent platform or vendor positions do not resolve those gaps. Unsupported claims were not promoted to satisfy a source-count target.
