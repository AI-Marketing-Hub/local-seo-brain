# Local SEO Brain Product Specification

## Buyer

Local SEO agencies and freelancers, multi-location operators, and in-house storefront, hybrid, or service-area operators who need a persistent evidence record and approval-first workflow.

## Promise

Turn authorized exports, current doctrine, and recurring decisions into a deterministic, source-cited Obsidian memory without mutating external accounts or inventing missing evidence.

## Shipped inputs

- manual evidence files
- Business Profile CSV exports
- citation CSV exports
- review JSON or CSV exports
- geo-grid CSV exports
- an optional operator-supplied historical seed, not shipped, qualified by current overlays

## Shipped outputs

- immutable, content-addressed raw receipts and a manifest
- source, location, citation, review-summary, and keyword-scan notes
- canonical claim and source ledgers plus deterministic Obsidian projections
- Draft Health Scorecard, Action Roadmap, Weekly Report, and Approval Queue
- strict repository and Obsidian lint evidence

## No-data behavior

Missing or insufficient inputs remain `Draft`, `no_data`, `needs_input`, `unsupported`, or `contested`. The product does not estimate a grade, ranking impact, traffic share, cadence, or cause.

## Safety and authority

Official policy controls policy claims. Empirical recommendations must state their scope and limitations. Account changes require owner approval and rollback. The product refuses credentials, private data in repository artifacts, address gaming, virtual-office abuse, fake or gated reviews, PBN tactics, doorway or hidden-text tactics, mass low-quality citation submission, prohibited Places storage, and guaranteed rankings.

## Version contract

`pyproject.toml` is the canonical version source. `marketplace.json`, `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, README, and CHANGELOG are validated projections of that version.
