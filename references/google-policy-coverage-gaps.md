# Google Policy Coverage Gaps

Reviewed 2026-10-15.

## Deliberately unresolved

- Manual place Q&A availability by locale, device, account, and category. The API sunset is verified; policy pages still discuss place questions and answers.
- Ask Maps behavior outside the documented initial U.S. and India mobile rollout, and the exact source mix used for any answer.
- Property-specific data sufficiency in the worldwide Search Console generative-AI impression report, and any future click, query, or conversion fields.
- Actual eligibility and participation for a particular EEA aggregator or supplier, including the applicable integration and contractual requirements.
- Enforcement frequency after Business Profile name, address, or category edits. Official sources say re-verification may occur but provide no rate.
- Local ranking effect sizes for reviews, categories, photos, posts, Core Web Vitals, structured data, or citations. Google provides no weights.
- Places content retention rules for every contemplated future adapter. Review the current terms and policy before implementation.

## Why these remain gaps

The reviewed sources do not provide reproducible, generalizable evidence. UI and API surfaces vary by rollout, locale, account, category, and device. Absence from documentation is not converted into a confident “never” claim. The product therefore records the gap, refuses universal advice, and schedules refreshes in the source ledger.

## Highest-value next evidence

Run a reproducible, locale-stratified observation harness for manual place Q&A and Ask Maps availability, with account/device/category sampling and no account mutation. Store only compliant observations and metadata; do not retain restricted Places payloads.
