# Local SEO Brain Research Plan

Status: maintenance review completed for the 2026-10-15 evidence snapshot. The machine freshness command evaluates this snapshot against a separate review date.

## Questions

1. What do current official Google Search sources say about eligibility, ranking systems, updates, AI features, structured data, and measurement?
2. What do current official Google Business Profile sources say about eligibility, representation, addresses, service areas, reviews, restrictions, appeals, features, and APIs?
3. What storage, caching, attribution, and use restrictions apply to Google Maps Platform and Places content?
4. Which historical quantitative, causal, cadence, and product-status claims have reproducible independent evidence, and what counter-evidence exists?
5. Which repository marketing claims are demonstrated by current code and tests?

## Exclusions

- No private account access, live account mutation, paid API calls, or client data egress.
- No whole-page copyrighted copies.
- No vendor opinion promoted to causal evidence without transparent methods.
- No provider/model churn in the core brain unless it changes a Local SEO decision.

## Source order

Controlling official policy and official product/API documentation come first. Standards define their own vocabularies. Transparent primary research and independent datasets may support empirical observations. Practitioner and community sources can identify workflows or questions but remain lower authority.

## Worker budget

At most four active agents including the lead. Each research worker is read-only and draft-only and owns a named subset of the source inventory. This maintenance pass reviewed all 66 existing sources and added four controlling primary sources. Workers report inspected URLs, dates, precise locators, contradictions, independence, and uncovered gaps. The lead canonicalizes URLs, resolves scope, and owns all writes.

## Stop conditions

Stop a question when controlling evidence is captured, additional sources repeat dependent evidence, the budget is exhausted, a critical fact cannot be verified, or further work would exceed approved domains or privacy boundaries. Record incomplete coverage rather than inventing certainty.

## Outputs

The outputs are `source-map.md`, `current-requirements.md`, `market-research.md`, `safety-gates.md`, canonical source and claim ledgers, a file coverage matrix, a contradiction/deprecation register, distilled canon entries, and cited vault source notes.
