# Uncertainty Evaluation Policy

## Operating contract

Evaluate whether an output distinguishes observed fact, policy, operational interpretation, practitioner judgment, contradiction, and unknown. Check that scope, source, date, assessment, confidence tag, and refresh deadline agree.

Missing data must not become a fabricated score, metric, cause, priority, or completed action. A failed or inconclusive evaluation remains visible.
