# Provenance Trace Policy

## Operating contract

An evidence-bearing output traces to a stable claim ID, source ID, canonical URL, locator, verification date, refresh date, and confidence tag. Raw client imports additionally record a content hash and immutable path.

Unknown dates and values remain unknown. Brief direct quotes require exact attribution; otherwise use a labeled paraphrase.
