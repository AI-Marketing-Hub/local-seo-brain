# Answer Engine Citability Policy

## Operating contract

Answer-engine visibility is not guaranteed by a file, schema type, citation count, platform mention, or universal recipe. Separate ordinary Search eligibility, platform-specific observation, and practitioner guidance. Every material answer claim needs a source, scope, date, assessment, and confidence tag.

Do not create fake community experience, undisclosed promotion, or fabricated attribution in pursuit of citations.
