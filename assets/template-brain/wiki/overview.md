# Overview

Local SEO Brain is an advisory Obsidian workspace for authorized local-SEO exports, evidence review, and human-approved recommendations. It does not mutate accounts or promise ranking outcomes.

![[brain-relationship-map.svg]]

## Data flow

1. An operator exports authorized GBP, citation, review, geo-grid, or site data.
2. An ingestor stores a content-addressed immutable raw receipt and descriptive derived notes.
3. The current source and claim ledgers constrain interpretation.
4. Synthesis produces Draft/no-data deliverables until the inputs support a scoped conclusion.
5. Any account, content, listing, outreach, or publishing action enters [[Approval Queue]].

The ingestors do not claim a NAP diff, sentiment model, causal diagnosis, competitor gap, grade, or finished action unless the implemented parser and supplied data actually produce it.

## Evidence boundary

The licensed 59-chapter source seed is preserved byte-for-byte under `.raw/source-material/`. Working chapters contain reviewed replacement guidance; original lesson bodies remain in the raw bundle. Uncited historical numbers and prescriptions are unsupported and cannot drive client work.

## Strategic frame

Property, GBP, and backlink/citation folders are navigation aids, not Google factor weights or a mandatory sequence. AI-search notes are platform-, query-, locale-, and date-scoped.
