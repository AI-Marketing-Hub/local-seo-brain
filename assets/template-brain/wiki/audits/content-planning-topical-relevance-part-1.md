# Content Planning: Topical Relevance Part 1

## Working guidance

Group supplied search queries and customer questions by the task they express. Draft page purposes without inventing search volume or conversion potential.

## Use this note

Inputs: Observed queries and customer questions, with their source, locale, date, and relevant business offering.

Result: Task-based topic groups and proposed page purposes, keeping unmeasured demand and competition unknown.

## Evidence and limits

Google documents no preferred universal word count, keyword density, publication cadence, or fixed page-count framework; content should be people-first and useful for its purpose. (Claim: no-universal-content-formula; sources: google-helpful-content, google-seo-starter-guide.)
