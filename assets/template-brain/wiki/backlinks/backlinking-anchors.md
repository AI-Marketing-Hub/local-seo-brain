# Backlinking Anchors

## Evidence status

- Assessment: **provisional**
- Confidence: **PRACTITIONER**
- Verified: **2026-10-15**
- Refresh due: **2026-11-14**
- Claim IDs: link-schemes-prohibited, no-universal-link-count, operator-evidence-review

## Working guidance

Inspect the supplied link sample for readable, relevant anchor text and paid or manipulative placements. Do not prescribe an exact-match ratio.

## Use this note

Inputs: A dated backlink sample with source pages, target URLs, anchor text, placement context, and any paid relationship.

Result: Anchor-context findings, qualification concerns, and evidence gaps, without a prescribed anchor ratio.

## Evidence and limits

PBNs, paid ranking links without qualification, optimized forum/comment links, and link networks created primarily to manipulate ranking violate Google's spam policies. (Claim: link-schemes-prohibited; sources: google-search-spam-policies, google-link-best-practices.)

Google documents no magical ideal internal or external link count; links should be useful, contextual, crawlable, and appropriately qualified. (Claim: no-universal-link-count; sources: google-link-best-practices, google-seo-starter-guide.)
