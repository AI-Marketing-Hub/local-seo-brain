# Key Google Rank Factors

## Evidence status

- Assessment: **contested**
- Confidence: **CONTESTED**

## Working guidance

Use Google’s public local-result framing to organize evidence. Do not assign private weights or attribute a site movement to an untested mechanism.

## Use this note

Inputs: The local-search surface, business location model, dated observations, and controlling ranking documentation.

Result: An evidence map under relevance, distance, and prominence, without weights or an invented cause for movement.

## Evidence and limits

Google publicly frames local results using relevance, distance, and prominence/popularity; these are not an exhaustive public formula. (Claim: local-ranking-factors; sources: google-local-ranking.)

Google does not publish universal weights, thresholds, or guaranteed lifts for local ranking factors. (Claim: local-ranking-no-public-weights; sources: google-local-ranking.)

Aggregated interaction data has been described in primary trial evidence, but this does not validate GA bounce-rate formulas, dwell-time thresholds, CTR manipulation, or local-ranking guarantees. (Claim: behavior-formulas-unsupported; contrary evidence: doj-google-interaction-testimony.)
