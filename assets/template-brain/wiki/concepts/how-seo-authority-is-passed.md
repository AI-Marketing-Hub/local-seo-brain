# How SEO Authority Is Passed

## Evidence status

- Assessment: **unsupported**
- Confidence: **FOLKLORE**
- Verified: **2026-10-15**
- Refresh due: **2026-11-14**
- Claim IDs: page-rank-no-fixed-model, no-universal-link-count, operator-evidence-review

## Working guidance

Use crawlable, descriptive links where they help readers. Do not calculate authority points or placement weights from an undocumented model.

## Use this note

Inputs: The source and destination pages, crawlable link markup, anchor context, and the customer navigation task.

Result: A useful-link assessment with observed crawlability and context, leaving undocumented authority arithmetic unsupported.

## Evidence and limits

Google documents evolved PageRank and link-analysis systems as only part of its ranking systems; fixed authority-point arithmetic and placement weights are unsupported. (Claim: page-rank-no-fixed-model; contrary evidence: google-search-ranking-systems, google-seo-starter-guide.)

Google documents no magical ideal internal or external link count; links should be useful, contextual, crawlable, and appropriately qualified. (Claim: no-universal-link-count; sources: google-link-best-practices, google-seo-starter-guide.)
