## Task

Draft a plain factual description of what the business does. Exclude unverified distinctions, prices, testimonials, or claims of superiority.

## Required inputs

Confirmed business name, operations, services, service area, and existing description.

## Output and acceptance

A Draft description with a sentence-to-fact mapping and unresolved inputs.

Tie factual assertions to supplied source IDs and locators. Retain needs_input for missing facts. The operator reviews scope, factual support, and privacy; any proposed external change needs owner approval and rollback.
