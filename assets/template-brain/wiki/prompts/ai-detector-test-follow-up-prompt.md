# Evidence and Style Review

> Draft prompt for [[content-blog-post-writing|Content: Blog Post Writing]]. Use only authorized evidence and review the result before external use.

## Task

Mark unsupported assertions and invented personal experience first. Then replace awkward, repetitive, or promotional phrasing while preserving factual meaning. Do not optimize for detector evasion.

## Required inputs

The Draft, its approved outline, source table, and intended audience.

## Output and acceptance

A revised Draft plus a change table separating factual corrections from style edits.

Tie factual assertions to supplied source IDs and locators. Retain needs_input for missing facts. The operator reviews scope, factual support, and privacy; any proposed external change needs owner approval and rollback.
