## Task

Group queries by the user task, map them to existing coverage, and identify genuinely distinct gaps. Mark demand and SERP overlap as unmeasured unless evidence is supplied.

## Required inputs

Supplied queries, customer questions, current pages, and business services.

## Output and acceptance

Intent groups, existing-page mappings, proposed page purposes, and evidence gaps.

Tie factual assertions to supplied source IDs and locators. Retain needs_input for missing facts. The operator reviews scope, factual support, and privacy; any proposed external change needs owner approval and rollback.
