# Metadata: SEO Titles and Descriptions

## Evidence status

- Assessment: **provisional**
- Confidence: **PRACTITIONER**
- Verified: **2026-10-15**
- Refresh due: **2026-11-14**

## Working guidance

Review the existing title and description against visible page content, page purpose, and verified business facts. Draft accurate, distinctive alternatives without promising which snippet Google will display.

## Use this note

Inputs: The visible page, existing title and description, page purpose, and confirmed business facts.

Result: Title/description alternatives with their factual basis and rationale; displayed snippets remain Google-controlled.

## Evidence and limits

Google recommends clear, concise, accurate, page-specific titles and short, unique meta descriptions. Search snippets can use page content rather than the supplied description. (Claim: metadata-accurate-descriptive; sources: google-seo-starter-guide.)
