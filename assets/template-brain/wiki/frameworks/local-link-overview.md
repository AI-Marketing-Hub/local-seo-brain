# Local Link Overview

## Evidence status

- Assessment: **accepted**
- Confidence: **EVIDENCE-BASED**
- Verified: **2026-10-15**
- Refresh due: **2026-11-14**
- Claim IDs: no-universal-link-count, link-schemes-prohibited, operator-evidence-review

## Working guidance

Prioritize legitimate local relationships and useful references. Reject artificial link quotas, ratios, and paid ranking schemes.

## Use this note

Inputs: Actual local organizations, business relationships, source pages, and the purpose of each proposed reference.

Result: Relevant relationship opportunities and legitimacy checks, with owner review before outreach.

## Evidence and limits

Google documents no magical ideal internal or external link count; links should be useful, contextual, crawlable, and appropriately qualified. (Claim: no-universal-link-count; sources: google-link-best-practices, google-seo-starter-guide.)

PBNs, paid ranking links without qualification, optimized forum/comment links, and link networks created primarily to manipulate ranking violate Google's spam policies. (Claim: link-schemes-prohibited; sources: google-search-spam-policies, google-link-best-practices.)
